# belajar html

hari ini say meanjutkan pembelajaran html saya di website w3schools.

penggunaan tag `<div>` untuk blok
```
 Lorem Ipsum <div>I am a div</div> dolor sit amet.
```

penggunaan tag `<div>` untuk bagian halaman web
```
<div>
  <h2>London</h2>
  <p>London is the capital city of England.</p>
  <p>London has over 9 million inhabitants.</p>
</div>
```

menyesuaikan tag `<div>` di css
```
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<style>
div {
  width: 300px;
  margin: auto;
  background-color: #FFF4A3;
}
</style>
<body>

<h1>Center align a DIV element</h1>

<div>
  <h2>London</h2>
  <p>London is the capital city of England.</p>
  <p>London has over 9 million inhabitants.</p>
</div>

</body>
</html>
```

penggunaan berbagai tag `<div>` dengan pewarnaan
```
<div style="background-color:#FFF4A3;">
  <h2>London</h2>
  <p>London is the capital city of England.</p>
  <p>London has over 9 million inhabitants.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#FFC0C7;">
  <h2>Oslo</h2>
  <p>Oslo is the capital city of Norway.</p>
  <p>Oslo has over 700,000 inhabitants.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#D9EEE1;">
  <h2>Rome</h2>
  <p>Rome is the capital city of Italy.</p>
  <p>Rome has over 4 million inhabitants.</p>
</div>
```

penggunaan float agar `<div>` sejajar
```
div.mycontainer {
  width:100%;
  overflow:auto;
}
div.mycontainer div {
  width:33%;  
  float:left;
}
</style>
<body>

<div class="mycontainer">

  <div style="background-color:#FFF4A3;">
    <h2>London</h2>
    <p>London is the capital city of England.</p>
    <p>London has over 9 million inhabitants.</p>
  </div>
```
